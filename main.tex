\begin{abstract}
  This paper examines the integration of neuro-symbolic artificial
  intelligence with industrial control systems, using pasteurization as a
  case study. We review the evolution of SCADA platforms from monolithic
  architectures to modern IoT-enabled systems, highlight the limitations of
  black-box neural controllers, and propose an ontological approach based on
  OSTIS Technology. The approach combines neural-network adaptability with
  symbolic reasoning and compliance with ISA-88, ISA-95, and ISA-5.1
  standards. The controller is implemented on a PLCnext Control AXC F~2152.
\end{abstract}
